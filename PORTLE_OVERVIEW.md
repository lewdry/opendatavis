# Portle

Portle is a minimum viable visualiser for open-source datasets.

## Why?

We love open-source data, but a range of constraints affecting government and non-profit organisations means it is often published with little effort: CSV and Excel files are dumped with limited descriptions, no view of schemas, sample data, or the shape of the data.

You're left deciding whether to download and parse a dataset based on a title and not much more.

## What it does

Portle gives a small snapshot of data without downloading it. Paste a URL to get a quick Chart.js view, with Portle making a best guess at how to display the data.
